Maia-3 uses the official CSSLab model and move vocabulary from
https://github.com/CSSLab/maia-platform-frontend/tree/a6e52f5c811ee18863cb2f0e81f2433a5b9905de
(GPL-3.0; included in MAIA-LICENSE.txt). `maia-moves.json` is the unmodified move vocabulary from that revision.
The ONNX model is downloaded from the same pinned revision at runtime.
Upstream license: https://github.com/CSSLab/maia-platform-frontend/blob/a6e52f5c811ee18863cb2f0e81f2433a5b9905de/LICENSE

ONNX Runtime Web 1.22.0 (MIT) is loaded from jsDelivr.
https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime

Existing dependencies: chess.js 1.4.0 (BSD-2-Clause), Stockfish.js 10.0.2
(GPL-3.0), and Wikimedia chess piece images.
